# IoT Copilot AI — Product Vision

## Vision Statement
Be the definitive AI-powered platform that transforms how beginners and intermediate learners master IoT — replacing scattered YouTube tutorials, forum posts, and documentation with a single, intelligent, personalized Senior IoT Engineer experience.

## Core Identity
- **Not a chatbot** — An experienced Senior IoT Engineer that teaches, guides, plans, remembers, and debugs
- **Agentic AI** — Proactive, context-aware, memory-driven, action-oriented
- **Platform** — Not just a tool; a complete learning ecosystem

## Brand Pillars
1. **Intelligence** — Powered by Groq AI that understands IoT deeply
2. **Guidance** — Step-by-step mentorship from zero to job-ready
3. **Memory** — Remembers every interaction, every project, every struggle
4. **Community** — Learn alone but grow together with peers

## Target Outcome
A beginner with zero knowledge can:
- Day 1: Understand what IoT is and get a personalized roadmap
- Week 1: Order the right components with AI recommendations
- Month 1: Build their first working IoT project
- Month 3: Debug complex hardware-software issues independently
- Month 6: Ace IoT job interviews with AI coaching
